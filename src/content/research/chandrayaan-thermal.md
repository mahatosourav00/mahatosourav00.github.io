## Master's thesis (2022–2023)

Near 3 µm, the sunlit Moon glows with its own heat, and that glow can hide or fake water and hydroxyl absorption. I built a **Python thermal-correction pipeline** for Chandrayaan-1 hyperspectral data, with **iterative Hapke photometric modelling** to refine surface-temperature estimates. Then I examined how OH/H₂O absorption is distributed across the lunar surface.

## Extension (2023–2024)

As a project intern I extended the method to include photometric modelling for **Chandrayaan-1 and Chandrayaan-2** data. I also generalized it so it can be applied to other airless bodies, which later fed directly into my Ceres work.
