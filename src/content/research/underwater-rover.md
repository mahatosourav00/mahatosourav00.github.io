## Underwater rover

- Built hardware for an underwater rover in the *Intelligent Vision System for Autonomous Health Monitoring of Underwater Oil Pipelines* project.
- Designed for **depths up to 100 m**: pressure housing, sensor integration and on-board automation.

## Low-cost BP monitor

- Modified existing digital medical devices to **extract raw physiological data**, the first step toward an open, low-cost blood-pressure monitoring system.
