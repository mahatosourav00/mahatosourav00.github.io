## Overview

Ceres is dark, salty and surprisingly varied. To compare its surface from place to place, the brightness changes caused by lighting geometry must first be removed. I worked on exactly that, using spectra from the Visible and InfraRed (VIR) mapping spectrometer on NASA's Dawn mission.

## Highlights

- Photometric modelling and **Hapke model corrections** to produce albedo maps of Ceres, in collaboration with the Max Planck Institute for Solar System Research.
- Analysed data from **five mission phases** and fitted phase curves to derive surface roughness and single-scattering albedo.
- Developed **artifact corrections**: despiking and mitigation of the spectral odd-even effect.
- Invited as a **guest scientist presenter** at the Ceres Workshop, MPS Göttingen (2024), supported by the MPG Ceres Fellowship.

## Outputs

Two manuscripts in preparation: *Spectrophotometric study of Ceres using VIR instrument data from DAWN mission* and *Artifact Correction of DAWN-VIR Instrument Data* (S. Mahato, G. Thangjam).
