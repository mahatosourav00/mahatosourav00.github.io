## The problem

Some of my friends work as community health officers in West Bengal. Part of their job is telemedicine: each month they must complete a set number of successful calls with doctors on the state's Swasthya Ingit portal. But the lines are almost always busy, and catching a doctor at the moment they become free is mostly luck. Reaching the monthly target was very hard, for reasons outside their control.

## What we built

A small browser extension for the portal. It shows which doctors are available, lets you filter them by status, and can **call a doctor automatically as soon as one becomes free**, so a health officer doesn't have to sit refreshing the page. It needs no technical skills to install or use.
