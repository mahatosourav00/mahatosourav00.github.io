This was my first team build, made as a beginner at the NISER Robotech Club. The rover was a simple, look-alike model of Chandrayaan-2's rover, built for an outreach event on the Chandrayaan-2 mission organised by NISER's School of Earth and Planetary Sciences. It was not a working replica.

- A **wirelessly controlled** rover prototype, inspired by ISRO's Pragyan.
- **Ultrasonic sensors**, **solar panels** and **real-time camera feeds** on board.

What I took away was the fundamentals of motors, drivers and sensors, and the confidence to start a build of my own: [an FPV drone](/projects/fpv-drone).

It ran at about the same time as the club's [maze-solving robot](/projects/maze-solving-robot), a separate project with a different goal.
