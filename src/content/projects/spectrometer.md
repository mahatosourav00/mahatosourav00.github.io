## Goal

Commercial spectrometers are expensive. I wanted to see how far a careful, mostly 3D-printed design could go for teaching and for lab studies of scattering.

## What I did

- Designed and printed the optical housing and mounts.
- Assembled and **calibrated** the instrument against reference sources.
- Used it to study how surface scattering changes with **geometry, roughness and temperature**.
