Not every useful program is clever. Some of the most useful ones just wait, so a person doesn't have to.

## The problem

Some of my friends work as **community health officers** in West Bengal. Part of their job is **telemedicine**: connecting with a doctor through a call on the state's **Swasthya Ingit** portal.

Every month, each health officer has to complete a set number of successful telemedicine calls. It's a mandatory part of the job.

The trouble is getting through. The calling traffic is almost always heavy, and the available doctors are almost always busy. Getting a call through meant sitting on the portal, watching the list of doctors, and trying again and again until someone happened to be free at exactly the right moment. Some months, reaching the target came down to luck and patience, for reasons completely outside my friends' control.

## The idea

The portal already showed which doctors were available. What it couldn't do was **wait** for you. So the idea was simple: a browser extension that sits on the portal page and does the waiting.

## What it does

- **Shows availability at a glance**: which doctors are available right now.
- **Filters doctors by status**, so the list shows only the ones worth trying.
- **Calls automatically**: as soon as a doctor becomes free, the extension places the call, so the health officer doesn't miss the moment.

It works in Chrome, Edge, Brave and other Chromium-based browsers, and it was written for people with no technical background. The install guide walks through every click, from unzipping the download to loading the extension.
